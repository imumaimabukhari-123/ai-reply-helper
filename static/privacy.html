Privacy

Last updated: October 2026

What the extension reads

Only the text you select on a page, and only when you click the extension's icon or choose Reply to this with AI from the right-click menu. It uses Chrome's activeTab permission, so it can't see your other tabs, your browsing history or anything else on the page.

What it sends

When you click Write 3 replies, the message, your chosen options and your optional detail are sent to this website's server, which asks Google Gemini to write the replies. Nothing is sent before you click.

What is stored

Messages and replies are not saved anywhere. The extension stores only your preferred tone, goal and length in your own browser. There are no accounts, no cookies and no tracking. To stop abuse, the server briefly counts requests per visitor (for the hourly limit) and forgets them after an hour.
