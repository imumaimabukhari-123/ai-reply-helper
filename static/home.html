How it works

Three clicks from message to reply.

1

Select the message

Highlight the text on any page, then click the 💬 icon, or right-click → Reply to this with AI.

2

Choose how to reply

Pick a tone (Professional, Friendly, Short, Formal, Enthusiastic) and what you want to say.

3

Copy your favourite

You get 3 different replies. Click Copy and paste it where you're replying.

Features

Small, fast and respectful of your privacy.

🎭

5 tones

From formal letters to quick friendly chats. Your last choice is remembered.

🎯

Say what you mean

Accept, politely decline, ask for details, say thanks, or "I'll get back to you".

📝

Add your details

Type a fact like "I'm free Friday after 2pm" and it goes into the reply. It never invents dates or prices.

🌍

Any language

A message in French, Urdu or Spanish gets replies in the same language.

🖱️

Right-click menu

Select text, right-click, and the helper opens with the message already filled in.

🔒

Private

It only reads the text you select, only when you ask. Nothing is stored.
